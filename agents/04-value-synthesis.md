# Value synthesis agent

## Input

A `TrialProfile`, a `CompetitorBrief`, and zero or more `AuditFinding` objects.

## Work

Produce a short first-week growth brief with a headline, up to three evidence-backed opportunities, clear uncertainty labels, and one ZenABM next step.

## Output

A `ValueBrief` that a marketer can read in under two minutes.

## Rules

- Prefer a smaller accurate brief to a complete-looking brief with weak evidence.
- Separate observed facts from interpretations and recommendations.
- Explain missing own-data or competitor coverage plainly.
