# Project brief

## User and moment

A new ZenABM trial user has just connected, or is close to connecting, their LinkedIn ads and optionally HubSpot. The first few days determine whether they see enough value to convert.

## Product promise

Give the user a concise first-week growth brief that shows something they can act on now:

- what their own LinkedIn/ABM data says, when it is available;
- what verified competitors appear to be doing publicly;
- which accounts or campaigns deserve attention; and
- the next ZenABM action that unlocks more value.

The brief should feel like a useful analyst deliverable, not generic AI copy.

## MVP

Input: one selected HubSpot trial contact or ZenABM account.

Output: a report, a HubSpot note/task, and email/LinkedIn-message drafts for human review.

Out of scope: automatic outreach, bulk contact processing, unverified competitor matches, and claims unsupported by data.

## Primary success signal

The trial user receives a useful, defensible finding and takes a meaningful in-product or sales-assisted next step.
